Delete your account

Last updated: 18 August 2026

The short version

This removes the anonymous space your purchase opened, and everything we hold in it. It does not touch your food diary on your phone — that stays where it is, and the app keeps working exactly like its free version.

You are leaving the server, not your diary.

From the app

Open SimplyCal, go to Settings → Backup, and choose Delete my online data. You will be asked to confirm. It takes effect immediately.

This is the reliable way, and on most phones the only one — see below.

If you no longer have the app

Write to miguel.dasilvagalvao1994@gmail.com with the order number of your purchase, which you will find in your Google Play receipt or under Payments and subscriptions → Budget and history. We reply within 30 days.

We ask for the order number because we hold no name and no e-mail address — there is no account, so there is nothing for us to look you up by. The order number is the only thing that connects a person to a stored space. It is also why deletion is built into the app itself: reinstalling and using Settings → Backup is faster and always works.

What is deleted

Immediately and permanently:

  • the anonymous identity your purchase opened;
  • the cloud copy of your foods, meals, daily entries, body measurements, goals and synced settings;
  • the photos you had attached to your own foods and meals.

What is not deleted

Your data on your phone. It never depended on the server, and it is not ours to delete. To remove it, uninstall the app.

Your subscription. It belongs to Google Play, not to us, and it keeps running — and billing — until you cancel it. Cancel it in the Play Store, under Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions, before erasing your data if you no longer want it.

Records Google Play keeps of a purchase. Tax and accounting law requires them to exist; they are held by Google, under its own terms.

If you change your mind

Nothing is recoverable once deleted — there is no thirty-day bin. Your subscription will open a fresh, empty space the next time the app checks it, and what ends up in it will be whatever your phone still holds.
