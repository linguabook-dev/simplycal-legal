Delete your account

Last updated: 18 August 2026

The short version

Deleting your account removes your account and everything we hold in the cloud. It does not touch your food diary on your phone — that stays where it is, and the app keeps working exactly like its free version.

You are leaving the server, not your diary.

From the app

Open SimplyCal, go to Settings, open your account, and choose Delete account. You will be asked to confirm. It takes effect immediately.

By e-mail

If you no longer have the app installed, write to miguel.dasilvagalvao1994@gmail.com from the e-mail address you signed in with, asking for your account to be deleted. We reply within 30 days, and in practice much sooner.

We ask you to write from that address because it is the only way to tell that the request comes from you. We will not delete an account on the word of an address we cannot connect to it.

What is deleted

Immediately and permanently:

  • your sign-in account;
  • the cloud copy of your foods, meals, daily entries, body measurements, goals and synced settings;
  • the photos you had attached to your own foods and meals.

What is not deleted

Your data on your phone. It never depended on the account, and it is not ours to delete. To remove it, uninstall the app.

Your subscription. It belongs to Google Play, not to us, and it keeps running — and billing — until you cancel it. Cancel it in the Play Store, under Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions, before deleting your account if you no longer want it.

Records Google Play keeps of a purchase. Tax and accounting law requires them to exist; they are held by Google, under its own terms.

If you change your mind

Nothing is recoverable once deleted — there is no thirty-day bin. You can sign in again with the same Google account, but you will be starting from an empty cloud, and what is restored will be whatever your phone still holds.
