SimplyCal Premium

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Who publishes SimplyCal

SimplyCal is published by NovaGEN Studio, based in British Columbia, Canada. Where this policy says « we », it means NovaGEN Studio.

In short

Your food diary lives on your phone. That is where it is written, and that is where the app reads it from — including if you pay.

There is no account. Nothing to create, no e-mail to give, no password to lose. If you subscribe to Premium, your purchase itself identifies you, and your diary is copied to our servers so that a new phone can restore it.

If you never buy anything, nothing about you ever reaches our servers, and the app works in full apart from that backup.

What the app stores on your device

Everything you enter is written to a database inside the app's own storage area on your device: the foods and meals you create, your daily entries, your weight and body-fat measurements, your profile (age, sex, height, activity level, goal) and your settings.

This is the original. The cloud copy, when it exists, is a copy. Uninstalling SimplyCal deletes the device copy; see below for the cloud one.

How you are identified — and what we never learn

When you subscribe, Google Play gives the app a purchase token. The app sends it to our server, which asks Google Play whether the purchase is genuine. If it is, the server creates an anonymous identity for that purchase and gives the app a session for it.

That identity is a random string. We never learn your name, your e-mail address, or your Google account — Google does not tell us, and we do not ask. Nothing we hold can be traced back to you by us. What ties the backup to you is the purchase, and only Google knows who made it.

The app remembers your last purchase token on the phone so it can recognise you again after a plan change or a re-subscription. That token never leaves the device except to be checked with Google.

Cloud backup (Premium)

If you are a Premium subscriber, the app copies to our servers: the foods and meals you created, your daily entries, your body measurements, your goals and the settings that are not specific to one phone. Photos you attached to your own foods and meals are copied too, reduced in size beforehand.

The theme, the display language, the reminder time and the subscription state are not copied: they belong to one phone.

The copy is stored with Google Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage, in the northamerica-northeast1 region (Montréal, Canada), in a space that only your own identity can read. Access is enforced by server-side security rules, not by the app.

It exists for one purpose: so that a lost, broken or replaced phone can get your diary back.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read nutrition labels and barcodes, and to photograph a food. The recognition runs entirely on your device, through Google ML Kit's on-device models; images captured for scanning are never uploaded.

Photos you attach to a food or a meal stay on your device, and are copied to the cloud only under the conditions described above.

The app asks for camera access only when you open a scanner, and it works without that permission — you can always type values in by hand.

Barcode lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode, the app sends that barcode number to Open Food Facts to fetch the product’s nutrition information. Only the barcode number is sent. No identifier of you, your device, or your diary is attached to that request.

Open Food Facts is an independent, non-profit database. Its own privacy policy governs that request, and its server records the usual technical information any web server receives, including your IP address. Product data returned by Open Food Facts is published under the Open Database Licence (ODbL).

Reminders

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled by your phone. Nothing is sent to a server, and no push service is involved.

Reports you export

The nutrition report is generated on your device as a PDF or a CSV file. You choose what happens to it. Once you share it — by e-mail, messaging or any other app — it is out of SimplyCal's hands and covered by whatever service you sent it through.

Subscriptions

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or your billing address.

The purchase token described above is kept for as long as the purchase is live, together with the anonymous identity it opened. That is what lets the same person find their backup again after changing phone, and what stops one purchase from opening several spaces. The token identifies a purchase, not a person.

Analytics and advertising

There are none. SimplyCal contains no advertising, no advertising identifier, and no behavioural analytics. Nothing about how you use the app is measured or transmitted.

How long we keep the cloud copy

While your subscription is active, we keep it so it can be restored.

When a subscription ends, the copy becomes read-only for 60 days: it stops receiving anything new, but a reinstall can still restore it. After those 60 days it is deleted automatically, along with the photos. Your device copy is never touched.

If you erase your online data, everything we hold is deleted immediately — see Delete your account.

Where your data is, and who processes it

The cloud copy is stored in Canada. Canada holds an adequacy decision from the European Commission, so data from the European Economic Area is transferred there without additional safeguards being required.

Our only processor is Google (Firebase Authentication — used only to carry the anonymous session — Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions), acting on our instructions. Google Play acts as merchant of record for purchases, under its own terms.

Children

SimplyCal is not directed at children. It is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act and comparable laws, you have the right to access, correct, export and erase your personal data, and to withdraw consent.

Most of them you exercise yourself, without asking us: the data is visible in the app, editable in the app, exportable as a report, erased on your device by uninstalling, and erased on our servers from Settings → Backup. If you have never subscribed, we hold nothing about you at all.

Because we hold no name and no e-mail address, we cannot find your data from an e-mail alone — the app is the only place that knows which identity is yours. That is a consequence of collecting nothing, and it is why deletion is built into the app itself. For anything else, write to the address below. We answer within 30 days. If you are in the EEA you may also complain to your national data protection authority; in Canada, to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the updated text will ship with the app update and the date at the top of this page will change. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
