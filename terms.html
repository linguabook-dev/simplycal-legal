What SimplyCal is

SimplyCal is a tool for recording what you eat and tracking calories, macronutrients and body measurements. It is a notebook that does arithmetic. It is not a medical device, a diagnostic tool, or a substitute for professional advice.

Not medical or dietary advice

The calorie and macronutrient figures the app suggests are estimates produced by the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and standard activity coefficients. They describe populations, not you. Your real needs depend on factors this app cannot measure.

Do not use SimplyCal to diagnose, treat or manage any medical condition. Speak to a doctor or a registered dietitian before starting any diet, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, have diabetes, kidney or heart disease, or take medication affected by diet.

If you have, or think you may have, an eating disorder, calorie tracking can make things worse. Please talk to a health professional rather than to an app.

Accuracy of nutrition data

Nutrition figures come from three places: a small built-in list, entries you create yourself, and the Open Food Facts database, which is contributed to by the public. None of these is guaranteed to be correct or current. Manufacturers change recipes, and labels are themselves subject to legal tolerances.

Always check figures that matter against the product's own label. You are responsible for what you record and for the decisions you take on the basis of it.

Scanning

Label and barcode scanning is a convenience, not a guarantee. Character recognition misreads figures, and a barcode may return the wrong product or none at all. Check anything the scanner fills in before you save it.
